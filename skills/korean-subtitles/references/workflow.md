# Language and evidence decisions

## Source and work understanding

Inventory only requested immediate media, excluding extras unless named. Inspect local/embedded sources first, then direct search/download (SUBDL, complementary Subf2m/SubSource/OpenSubtitles as needed). Names/tags/uploader claims are leads. Verify actual language/profile, cut correspondence, completeness, distinctive anchors and discontinuities. Retain raw originals; explicitly convert malformed/encoded material into a separate documented copy. Stop acquisition when a sufficient pair exists. Never execute downloads or use browser automation to bypass access failure.

Complete collection for the whole admitted request first. The coordinator then studies the identified work before any corrected text is written. Start with official work information and Wikipedia work/season material, then matching episode references and material gaps. Actually inspect bodies; record unavailable access separately from inspected absence. Reuse an inspected document/version, never repeatedly research it for each episode. Work identity, plot/causal order, character goals/knowledge/relations/actual address patterns, register, recurring terms, reveal chronology and intentional uncertainty belong in concise source-linked shared facts, not separate category essays. The selected English controls the actual edit and cue-level revelation, subject to source errors established by sufficient evidence as described below.

Use targeted English source reading to fill a necessary study gap; expand to the whole source only when needed. No mandatory full E/K preread. The LLM explicitly judges `ready` or `needs_evidence`; code records that declaration and checks its consistency/bindings, not understanding. Necessary unanswered questions prevent readiness; intentional ambiguity or immaterial unknowns do not. Share completed study before assigning correction. During correction read every selected English/Korean cue, including effects, Korean-only and unmatched cues, while producing corrected Korean. Validate/refine prior facts against actual dialogue; update genuine discrepancies and semantic impact, without repeating the study or writing a new full synopsis.

For consequential names/forms, inspect applicable evidence when needed. A collected original's Korean form may be retained when a different form is not established; record meaningful discrepancy and uncertainty rather than treating English spelling alone as proof of a Korean form. Correct established defects. Identity, Korean form and currently revealed alias are separate language questions for author and reviewer. The engine verifies references and versions, not correct spelling, coreference or meaning.

When actual evidence suggests a selected-English error, inspect the original and surrounding context, then only the necessary other versions or applicable official evidence. Keep upload labels and claimed release provenance distinct from verified origins; duplicate or related copies are not independent confirmations. Distinguish an established source error from a mere discrepancy, and an established correction from an uncertain alternative. Record the actual evidence and limitations in shared context; when supported, correct Korean output without changing selected-original bytes, cue timing or surviving structure. If an alternative is not established, assess the collected Korean and context without inventing certainty; hold an unresolved material issue. If faithful output cannot retain source structure, hold. Existing song identity and artist verification rules remain in force. The independent reviewer checks the evidence, corrected output and declared semantic impact; code records the declarations, not their truth.

## Translation

The English SSOT is the output anchor; collected Korean is translation material, including unchanged text. Match E/K by meaning, not numbers or timing. During the same bilingual pass apply this criterion:

Apply the SSOT authority principle in SKILL.md using actual work understanding, not lexical substitution rules. Establish what this source exchange communicates or excludes at its current reveal point, then compare what Korean actually communicates. Preserve source naming/address/reference choices and function; do not redesign them around Korean social or kinship conventions. Correct source-excluded interpretations introduced by Korean or source uncertainty removed by Korean. Preserve justified ellipsis when it retains immediate equivalent understanding of the source. Natural wording cannot override source choices or supply absent distinctions. No word-specific exception list, back-translation, alternative draft or per-cue assurance replaces this judgment.

Check all meaning, conditions/negation, language, spelling/spacing, register, omissions/duplication and terms. Naturalness/professional provenance does not establish fidelity. Correct every established defect without change-count limits.

Preserve surviving SSOT intervals/order/line/speaker/emphasis structure. Emphasis correspondence is semantic; code cannot infer it from character offsets. Expressive punctuation/function survives without imposing English grammar. Classify each source portion by meaning: retain dialogue/lyrics/identified songs, exclude only nonverbal effects/atmosphere/speaker labels. Parentheses are not automatic effects. Hold faithful Korean that cannot meet the fixed structure; do not retime/reflow to avoid rewriting.

## Observation and music

Respect viewer-facing Korean-only material from the collected original, including screen translation and dialogue. English silence alone does not make it an error or require audiovisual investigation; preserve its original timing when practicable. Compare the surrounding English and Korean to prevent actual duplication, contradiction or premature reveal; correct established defects. Attribute it to the collected original, not audiovisual observation. When overlapping dialogue, preserve both: use top positioning only after verifying the target player's support with a synthetic example; otherwise combine or minimally adjust timing while preserving meaning. Do not change player settings. Record actual perception separately from inference; a still cannot verify speech. No automatic OCR/ASR, exhaustive frames or claimed listening from extraction.

For one document/chat/title event, retain one reusable evidence object with exact original field text and each field's first verified readable time/observed hold. Supplements reference fields and stay inside those intervals. LLM judgment establishes readability and appropriate duration; numeric interval tests do not. Hashes identify exact artifacts, not equivalent scenes. Each independent reviewer inspects evidence actually needed; the coordinator does not habitually inspect it again.

Include all transcribed singing/refrains/vocal syllables and source song identity. Do not retrieve missing lyrics. If artist is absent, inspect official OST first, then applicable official film/studio credits when needed. Separate access failure from inspected absence. Verify the actual used version; composer/album credit/original performer/unrelated cover is insufficient. Reuse the inquiry per song/version. Unsupported artist attribution stays absent with the limitation reported.

## Direct independent comparison and revision

After shared study, the first corrector reads all English/Korean while correcting and seals completed correction before handover. Another actual worker directly compares all E/K/O/S, including unchanged text, excluded English and unmatched Korean, with the same factual study/current evidence. Assess fidelity, natural Korean within source choices, comprehension and cue-time knowledge together. No Korean-only prereading or simulated ignorance stage. Receipts bind scope, not understanding or real independence.

Episodes enter review independently; other episodes need not finish. A worker may review a different episode they did not first-correct. The same episode's first corrector cannot approve it. Reviewers directly correct established defects, including whole passages where needed; only unresolved questions need findings. A source-known reviewer must still judge what the Korean actually communicates at presentation, rather than mentally supplying omitted source information. Preserve justified ellipsis and intended uncertainty, never normalize future identity into earlier dialogue.

Code binds changed anchors/correspondences; the reviewer judges additional semantic impact. Confirm the current compiled changed/affected scope and resolve findings before approval; no writer-return/reseal roundtrip for local corrections. Packet membership is not a dependency edge. Unclear impact → episode reassessment; true causal/relationship/reveal premise correction → global reassessment. Newly learned information, metadata additions and editorial refinements are not automatically global invalidation. Existing shared-premise changes require an explicit evidence-grounded impact assessment, which independent review also checks.

Shared context is source-grounded supporting evidence, never an answer key or protected interpretation. Do not provide the first corrector’s prose understanding/verdict to the reviewer; derive understanding from the originals and independently inspect current factual evidence. Reuse metadata/evidence by current entry; retirement notices preserve history without treating past claims as current truth. Final coverage binds current output and evidence. Coordinator integration addresses actual cross-episode conflicts and their consumers, not another whole-season read. Stop unsupported consequential judgments rather than guessing or adding universal incident rules.

## Bounded text patches and placement

A user-authorized bounded patch uses exact deployed baseline, declared before/after text and required E/K context. It never relabels the baseline as collected Korean or imports approval. Preserve every byte outside authorized text spans. Baseline structure must correspond to SSOT; a structural/timing change requires hold/full preparation.

Only `finalize` places after current input/target/evidence checks, independent coverage/resolution and exact approval. Preflight all batch outputs before first write. Per-file replacement is atomic, a batch is not a transaction. Inspect targets and partial results after a failure before retrying. No backup/rollback/migration/media or IINA changes.

Operational state and packet contracts use the v7 SQLite workflow in records.md. Language/evidence judgments in this document are unchanged.
